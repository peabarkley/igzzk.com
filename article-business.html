Business Tools

How to switch to a business account (and actually read your insights)

9 min readUpdated September 2026

If you're still on a personal profile, switching to a business account unlocks the tools you actually need to grow: Insights, contact buttons, ad capabilities, and Meta Business Suite. Here's how to do it, and what to do with the data once you have it.

How to switch to a business account

The process takes about two minutes. Here's the exact path:

  • Open your Instagram profile and tap the three lines (hamburger menu) in the top-right corner.
  • Go to Settings and privacy.
  • Tap Account type and tools.
  • Select Switch to professional account.
  • Choose the category that best describes you (e.g., "Local business," "Digital creator").
  • Select Business (not Creator — Business is better suited for companies, Creator is for individual influencers).

Once you're set up, connect your Facebook Page. This is required for running ads through Meta, and it unlocks Meta Business Suite for managing both platforms together.

What you unlock

  • Instagram Insights — your analytics dashboard
  • Contact buttons — email, phone, and directions on your profile
  • Stories link support — add links to Stories regardless of follower count
  • Meta Business Suite — schedule posts, manage messages, and run ads across Facebook and Instagram
  • Professional Dashboard — track reach, engagement, follower growth, and content performance

How to read your Insights

Instagram's official Help Center explains that the Professional Dashboard is a "centralized hub for all public accounts" that shows your account performance and gives you access to every related tool. For professional accounts, you tap Professional Dashboard under your bio. View source ↗

From there, you can track three core areas:

  • Reach — how many unique accounts saw your content. Instagram defines it as "the number of unique accounts that saw your content on screen at least once, regardless of whether it played." View source ↗
  • Engagement — how many people interacted with your posts. Instagram splits this into two numbers: interactions (total actions like likes, comments, saves, shares) and accounts engaged (the unique accounts behind those interactions).
  • Audience demographics — where your followers are from and who they are. Instagram notes these are estimates and that you need at least 100 accounts in a given period for the demographic data to appear.

The dashboard also shows a 30-day performance summary. You can tap "view all insights" for more detailed metrics.

What the numbers actually mean

Instagram's official Insights overview makes one distinction that trips up most people: views and reach are not the same number. Views count every time your content was displayed, including repeat views from the same account. Reach counts unique accounts. If one person sees your post four times, that's four views but one reach. View source ↗

For a deeper breakdown, read our full guide on Instagram reach vs impressions.

Competitive Insights

Instagram has also introduced a Competitive Insights feature that lets you track competitor accounts directly in the app. Instagram hasn't published a dedicated Help Center page for this feature yet, so the exact list of accounts and metrics you can track may vary by region and account type.

What to do with this

Switch to a business account today — it's free and takes two minutes. Check your Professional Dashboard once a week. Track reach and sends, not likes. And remember: views and reach are different numbers. Views count displays; reach counts people.
