Instagram account recovery: every problem, solved

Locked out, restricted, disabled, or stuck on a verification screen? Instagram has no support phone number, and the old email no longer works. Here's every recovery problem — and the exact fix for each.

Before you dig in

Find your problem first

Recovery paths differ depending on what actually happened. Open your Instagram app and check Settings → Account Status before reading any guide. Green means you're fine — your issue is likely verification or 2FA. Red means you're dealing with a restriction or suspension.

Before anything else

Four things not to do

These apply to every recovery scenario on this page. Breaking any of them makes recovery harder.

01

Don't open a new account

Instagram can flag it as evading a ban, and that makes recovering the original account harder.

02

Don't submit five appeals

You appeal once, properly. Multiple submissions can get your case deprioritized in the review queue.

03

Don't pay a "recovery service"

Instagram has no support phone number. Meta has taken legal action against fake recovery services. They are scams.

04

Don't share your password, OTP, or backup codes

No legitimate Instagram employee will ever ask for them. Anyone who does is attempting to take over your account.

The big picture

How Instagram recovery actually works

Instagram's official support path has narrowed over the years. The company removed phone support entirely, and the old support@instagram.com email no longer handles account appeals. What's left is a set of self-service flows inside the app and on the web.

There are three core paths, and which one applies depends on what happened to your account:

  • In-app appeal (for disabled accounts). If you see a suspension notice when you try to log in, the app itself shows an appeal button. This is the preferred path.
  • Hacked account flow (for compromised accounts). If your account was taken over rather than disabled, start at instagram.com/hacked.
  • Verification flows (for lockouts). If you can't log in because of a verification code or 2FA issue, the recovery happens through backup codes, trusted contacts, or identity verification.

Each of the five guides above walks through one of these paths in detail.

After you're back in

Four things to do immediately

Recovery is not the end. Once you have access again, lock the account down so this doesn't happen twice.

01

Turn on two-factor authentication

The single most effective way to prevent future takeovers. Use an authenticator app, not SMS.

02

Save your backup codes

Instagram gives you one-time backup codes when you enable 2FA. Store them somewhere you'll find them if you're locked out again.

03

Review linked apps

Go to Settings → Apps and Websites and remove anything you don't recognize. Third-party automation apps are a common cause of suspensions.

04

Change your password

If you suspect any compromise at all, change the password and log out of all devices.
