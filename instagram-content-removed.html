Instagram removed your post? How to appeal

3 min readUpdated November 2026

Instagram's Help Center says it removes a post, comment, or story when it finds that the content goes against the Community Guidelines. Those guidelines apply worldwide. Instagram also says it does not always get that decision right, and that you can ask for another look in most cases. View source ↗

Ask for a review

There are a few ways to ask. When the violation notification explains why the content was removed, tap Ask for a review at the bottom and follow the on-screen instructions. You can also start from Account Status or Support Inbox. View source ↗

For some types of content, Instagram does not offer a review. It says it is adding more options.

From Account Status

Instagram publishes this path in the app:

  1. Tap Profile or your profile picture in the bottom right.
  2. Tap Menu in the top right.
  3. Under More info and support, tap Help.
  4. Tap Account Status, then tap Removed content.
  5. Tap Removed content.
  6. Tap the content that was removed, then tap Request a review.
  7. Select the reason you want another review, then tap Continue.
  8. Tap Submit, then tap Done.

From Support Inbox

  1. Tap Profile or your profile picture in the bottom right.
  2. Tap Menu in the top right.
  3. Under More info and support, tap Help.
  4. Tap Support Requests, then tap Violations.
  5. Open the update about the decision.
  6. Tap See options at the bottom.
  7. Tap Request review, then follow the on-screen instructions.

You can check the request later in Account Status, or by opening the violation in Support Inbox.

What happens after you ask

Instagram says another look uses both technology and people. Some appeals are prioritized for human review, which limits how many requests it can look at. Because of that, it may take up to 90 days to hear back, and the request may be closed before it is reviewed. View source ↗

When Instagram makes a decision, it sends a push notification. If it decides it made a mistake, it says it will tell you and the content will be reposted.

A first-time strike is a separate step

You may be able to remove a strike if this is the first time in the past year that Instagram removed content for not following its Community Standards. During that process, Instagram asks you to read about the policies. You can also say what you think happened. Instagram's own example is "I didn't know about the rules." View source ↗

The current taps are on that Help Center page, under Account Status. After you remove the strike, Instagram says:

  • The strike is removed from the account.
  • The content stays down. Removing the strike does not put it back.
  • You will not get another chance to remove a strike for a year.
  • You may still request a review if you want the content restored.

If you do not see the option, Instagram says it may be because this is not your first strike within a year, or because this strike is not eligible to be removed. Account Status is also where Instagram says you can see content taken down for violating Community Standards.

Oversight Board, after the review

If you still disagree after a review, you may be able to appeal to the Oversight Board. You have to request a review first. If Instagram has reviewed the content twice and you still disagree, you may be eligible. Not every content decision is eligible, and Instagram says it is adding more options. View source ↗

The board chooses only some of the eligible appeals. You have 15 days from when the decision was made. After that, Instagram says the window has expired. You can check the appeal on the Oversight Board's website with the reference number.

What to do with this

Request a review from the violation notification, Account Status, or Support Inbox, then check those same places for the result. If this was a first removal in the past year, the strike-removal option is separate, and it leaves the content down. A disabled account uses a different appeal.

If the account itself was disabled, use the disabled-account guide or the appeal guide. Otherwise go back to the Help Center map.
