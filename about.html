About

We translate official docs, so you don't have to.

IGZZK started with a simple frustration: Instagram's Help Center is thorough, but it reads like legal text. The rules are there, but finding them and understanding what they actually mean for your account takes hours.

So we built a knowledge base that does one thing: take every official Instagram source — Help Center articles, Business Tools documentation, API references, product announcements — and translate it into plain English. No jargon. No "insider tricks". No rumours.

What we cover

  • Algorithm Decoded — how Feed, Reels, Stories, and Explore ranking actually works.
  • How to Post — step-by-step guides for every content format.
  • Business Tools — Insights, Promote, Shopping, and Meta Business Suite walkthroughs.
  • Pitfalls to Avoid — account recovery, original content rules, and the stuff the platform won't tell you directly.

Our principles

  • Official sources only. If it's not from Instagram or Meta, we don't publish it as fact.
  • Plain language first. We avoid jargon. If we can say "how many people saw it", we will.
  • Updated regularly. Instagram changes its rules multiple times a year. We follow every update.
