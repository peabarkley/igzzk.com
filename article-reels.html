How long can Instagram Reels be?

8 min readUpdated October 2026

Instagram allows you to record and upload Reels up to 20 minutes long. But to actually reach people who don't follow you, the limit is much shorter: 3 minutes.

Here's the complete breakdown of Instagram Reels length limits, file sizes, and resolution specs — straight from the official Help Center — plus what you need to know about the algorithm that distributes them.

Instagram Reels Length Limits (2026)

There are two different limits you need to know, and conflating them is a common mistake:

  • Absolute maximum: 20 minutes. You can upload a video up to this length, but it will only be shown to your existing followers.
  • Recommendation limit: 3 minutes. Instagram's official Help Center states that Reels over 3 minutes will not be recommended to new audiences. View source ↗

Instagram Reels Size & Specs

If you're uploading from outside the app, you need to hit these technical specs:

Spec Official Requirement
Resolution 1080 x 1920 pixels (9:16 aspect ratio)
Minimum resolution 720 pixels
Frame rate At least 30 FPS
Maximum file size 4 GB
Cover image size 420 x 654 pixels (1:1.55 aspect ratio)
Accepted aspect ratios Between 1.91:1 and 9:16

If your video doesn't match these specs, Instagram will compress or crop it, which can hurt your video quality. View source ↗

Why the 3-Minute Limit Matters

If your goal is just to share a long video with friends, 20 minutes is fine. But if you want the algorithm to push your video to new people, you have to stay under 3 minutes. The Instagram Reels algorithm prioritizes watch time and completion — a 15-second Reel watched all the way through twice performs better than a 60-second Reel that gets abandoned at 10 seconds.

What the Instagram Reels Algorithm Rewards in 2026

Adam Mosseri confirmed the three most important signals for Reels distribution in January 2025: watch time, sends per reach, and likes per reach.

Watch time

How long someone actually watches your Reel. Instagram's official ranking documentation lists completion rate as one of the primary predictions for Reels distribution. A 15-second Reel watched all the way through twice outperforms a 60-second Reel that gets abandoned at 10 seconds.

Sends per reach

How often people share your Reel via direct message. For reaching people who don't follow you yet, sends carry more weight than likes. This is the signal that separates content people genuinely want to pass along from content they passively scroll past.

Likes per reach

Still one of the top three signals. For content shown to your existing followers, likes matter slightly more than sends. For discovery to non-followers, sends are more important. This distinction matters: if your goal is to reach new people, optimize for shareability.

What Changed in 2026 vs. 2025

Three shifts in the past year have changed how the Instagram Reels algorithm distributes content:

  • Recommendation Eligibility tightened. Instagram now has a formal concept called Recommendation Eligibility. If your content violates the platform's recommendation guidelines, it won't be recommended to people who don't follow you — it will only be shown to your existing followers. You can check your account's eligibility in Settings → Account Status. View source ↗
  • The definition of "unoriginal content" became explicit. Instagram's official recommendation guidelines now define unoriginal content as content that "largely reworks information from other sources, but with only minor and insignificant edits, without adding substantive value." View source ↗
  • The 3-minute recommendation threshold was confirmed. Instagram's official Help Center now states that Reels over 3 minutes will not be recommended to new audiences. View source ↗

Why the First 3 Seconds Decide Your Reels Reach

Instagram's official ranking documentation describes a specific prediction: likelihood you'll watch less than 3 seconds of a reel — framed as a negative or "bail-out" prediction. The signals include how often viewers skip within the first few seconds of opening a Reel. Instagram explicitly models and penalizes videos with a fast bail-out rate as a first-class ranking input.

Original audio vs. trending sounds

Instagram's official recommendation guidelines list unoriginal content as a factor that affects recommendation eligibility. View source ↗

Trending audio can still work, but Reels that rely entirely on someone else's sound carry more risk than content with original audio. If you use trending audio, pair it with original content.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long can Instagram Reels be?

Instagram allows Reels up to 20 minutes long. But Instagram's official Help Center states that Reels over 3 minutes will not be recommended to new audiences. For maximum discovery, keep Reels under 3 minutes.

What is the best resolution and file size for Instagram Reels?

The recommended resolution is 1080 x 1920 pixels (9:16 aspect ratio). The minimum resolution is 720 pixels, and the frame rate must be at least 30 FPS. The maximum file size is 4 GB.

What is the Instagram Reels algorithm?

The Instagram Reels algorithm is a recommendation system that decides which Reels appear in the Reels tab, Explore, and other users' feeds. Instagram has confirmed that it predicts how likely you are to reshare a Reel, watch it all the way through, like it, and go to the audio page.

Why do my Reels get no views on a new account?

Instagram's ranking system tests every Reel with a small initial audience. If those viewers watch to the end, share, or rewatch, distribution expands. If they scroll past in the first few seconds, distribution stops. Low views on a new account usually means the opening seconds aren't holding attention.

What to do with this

Keep Reels under 3 minutes for discovery. Export your videos at 1080x1920 and 30 FPS. Optimize for watch time and sends, not just likes. Front-load the hook in the opening seconds. And review your Insights to see which Reels are earning sends.
