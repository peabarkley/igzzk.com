Before you write

We read everything. Here's what helps.

The faster we understand your message, the faster we can act on it. Here are the four things people contact us about most — and what to include for each.

✓

Report an error

Link to the article and quote the exact sentence. If you have an official source that contradicts us, send the link.

✓

Suggest a topic

Describe what you're trying to understand, not just the topic name. "How do I know if my account is shadowbanned" is more useful than "shadowban".

✓

Request a correction

Same as reporting an error. Include the official source, and we'll update the article with a note.

✓

Press or partnership

Tell us who you are, what you're working on, and what you need from us. We'll respond if there's a fit.
