Privacy Policy

Here's exactly what we collect, why we collect it, and what we never do with it. No legal maze — just plain English.

Sept 2026last updated
5 minreading time

What we collect

When you subscribe to our newsletter, we collect your email address. When you browse the site, standard server logs may record your IP address, browser type, and pages visited. That's it.

How we use it

We use your email address only to send you the updates you subscribed to. We use server logs to understand which articles are useful and to keep the site secure. We don't build advertising profiles, and we don't run behavioural tracking.

What we don't do

  • We do not sell your email address.
  • We do not share it with third parties for marketing purposes.
  • We do not track you across other websites.
  • We do not use advertising cookies or third-party tracking cookies.

Cookies

This site uses minimal cookies for basic functionality. There are no ad network cookies, no cross-site tracking pixels, and no data brokers involved anywhere in this site.

Your rights

You can unsubscribe from our newsletter at any time by clicking the link in any email we send. You can request that we delete your email address from our records by contacting us. If you're in the EU or UK, you have the right to access, correct, or delete your data under GDPR. If you're in California, you have similar rights under CCPA.

Data retention

We keep your email address for as long as you're subscribed. If you unsubscribe, we remove it from our active list. Server logs are retained for 30 days, then automatically purged.

Third-party services

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. If you subscribe to our newsletter, your email address is stored with our email service provider. We choose providers that comply with GDPR and do not sell subscriber data.

Changes to this policy

If we make material changes to this policy, we'll update the "last updated" date at the top of this page. We won't notify you by email for minor changes — so if privacy matters to you, check back occasionally.

Contact

If you have questions about this policy, you can reach us through our contact page.

Instagram changed something?
We'll tell you first.

Every time the platform tweaks its rules or launches a new feature, we send you a plain-English breakdown. No spam, unsubscribe anytime.

We respect your privacy. We will never share your email.
