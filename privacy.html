Privacy Policy

Here's exactly what we collect, why we collect it, and what we never do with it. No legal maze — just plain English.

Sept 2026last updated
5 minreading time

What we collect

When you subscribe to our newsletter or send us a message through our contact form, we collect your name and email address. When you browse the site, standard server logs may record your IP address, browser type, and pages visited. We also use Google Analytics to understand how the site is used — see the Cookies and analytics section below. That's it.

How we use it

We use your email address only to send you the updates you subscribed to, or to reply to your message. We use server logs and analytics data to understand which articles are useful and to keep the site secure. We don't build advertising profiles, and we don't run behavioural tracking.

What we don't do

  • We do not sell your email address.
  • We do not share it with third parties for marketing purposes.
  • We do not track you across other websites.
  • We do not use advertising cookies or third-party tracking pixels.

Cookies and analytics

This site uses Google Analytics to understand which articles are useful and where visitors come from. Google Analytics sets cookies (_ga, _gid) that record anonymized usage data — pages visited, time on page, referring source, and general geographic region. IP addresses are anonymized.

We do not use advertising cookies, we do not run cross-site tracking pixels, and we do not sell or share this data with data brokers.

You can opt out of Google Analytics tracking by installing the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

Formspree

Our contact form and newsletter signup are processed by Formspree, a third-party form service. When you submit a form, your name, email address, and message are transmitted to and stored by Formspree on our behalf. Formspree's privacy policy governs their handling of that data. We only use it to receive and respond to your message.

Your rights

You can unsubscribe from our newsletter at any time by clicking the link in any email we send. You can request that we delete your email address from our records by contacting us. If you're in the EU or UK, you have the right to access, correct, or delete your data under GDPR. If you're in California, you have similar rights under CCPA.

Data retention

We keep your email address for as long as you're subscribed. If you unsubscribe, we remove it from our active list. Server logs are retained for 30 days, then automatically purged. Google Analytics retains data for 14 months by default.

Third-party services

This site is hosted on Vercel, with DNS managed by Cloudflare. If you subscribe to our newsletter or send us a message through the contact form, your data is stored with Formspree. Site usage is analyzed by Google Analytics. We choose providers that comply with GDPR and do not sell subscriber data.

Changes to this policy

If we make material changes to this policy, we'll update the "last updated" date at the top of this page. We won't notify you by email for minor changes — so if privacy matters to you, check back occasionally.

Contact

If you have questions about this policy, you can reach us through our contact page.

Instagram changed something?
We'll tell you first.

Every time the platform tweaks its rules or launches a new feature, we send you a plain-English breakdown. No spam, unsubscribe anytime.

We respect your privacy. We will never share your email.
