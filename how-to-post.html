Key takeaways

What actually matters

If you only remember four things from this section, remember these.

01

Portrait 4:5 is the new default

1080 x 1350 pixels. Takes up more screen real estate than square and displays correctly everywhere.

02

Reels: keep them short

15–45 seconds is the sweet spot. Reels over 3 minutes won't get recommended to new audiences.

03

5 hashtags, max

Instagram now enforces a hard limit. Pick niche community tags over mega-tags.

04

Hook in the first 2 seconds

For Reels especially, the first few seconds decide whether the algorithm keeps testing your content.
