Editorial Policy

Sources

Every factual claim on this site is sourced from Instagram's Help Center, Meta's Business Tools documentation, official Instagram product announcements, or public statements from Instagram leadership (such as Adam Mosseri's reels and the @creators account).

Verification

Before publishing, every article is checked against the original source. If Instagram's official documentation changes, we update the article and note the change.

What we don't publish

  • Unverified "growth hacks" or "insider tricks" that cannot be traced to an official source.
  • Rumours presented as fact.
  • Paid placements or sponsored content disguised as editorial.

Corrections

If you find an error, please let us know. We correct mistakes promptly and transparently.
