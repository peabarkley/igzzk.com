Key takeaways

What actually matters

If you only remember four things from this section, remember these.

01

Sends beat likes

A DM share counts more than any other engagement signal. Make content people want to send to a friend.

02

Watch time is king

How long someone actually watches matters more than how many people tapped play. Keep it short and front-load the hook.

03

Original always wins

Watermarked reposts get down-ranked. Photos and carousels are protected too, not just Reels.

04

Interest > follower count

The platform recommends based on topic, not just on who follows you. Niche beats broad.
