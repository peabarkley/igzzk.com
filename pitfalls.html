Key takeaways

What actually matters

01

No phone support exists

Anyone offering to "unban" your account for money is a scam. Meta has taken legal action against these operations.

02

Appeal once, in-app

Don't submit five appeals. The in-app path is preferred and links directly to your account's review queue.

03

Watermarks don't make it original

Adding a logo or slowing down someone else's video doesn't count. Visual fingerprinting detects near-duplicates.

04

Turn on 2FA after recovery

The single most effective way to prevent future account takeovers.
